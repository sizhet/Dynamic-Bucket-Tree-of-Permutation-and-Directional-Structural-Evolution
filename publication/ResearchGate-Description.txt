D-BTP: Dynamic Bucket Tree of Permutation — Directional Structural Generation and Hypothesis Evolution (v1.0.0)

D-BTP is a research framework that extends Bucket Tree of Permutation (BTP) from structural metric similarity and unaligned matching toward dynamic, constraint-guided structural hypothesis search. Rather than relying solely on a fixed candidate list, D-BTP allows candidate relationships and structural hypotheses to emerge from evolving bucket states. Its central proposal couples structural generation with directional selection: directional fields influence which hypotheses are generated, evaluated, and explored, while evidence and feedback may revise subsequent search guidance. The framework distinguishes hard constraints from uncertain or weak evidence, supports competing hypotheses and counterevidence, and explores multi-agent Structural Relay, collective learning, and carefully validated recursive improvement. The v1.0.0 research release comprises eight foundations papers, four specifications, three illustrative cases, and three extensions, together with open questions, testable hypotheses, and a validation roadmap. Applications discussed include dynamic starmap similarity and blockchain behavioral actor attribution; the latter does not equate structural attribution with identification of real-world individuals. The proposed mechanisms and potential performance advantages remain research hypotheses requiring controlled benchmarking and independent validation. The project is intended as an open, collective-learning-friendly research foundation, not a claim of demonstrated general intelligence or validated RSI.
